top3_by_region <- ice_cream |> 
  group_by(region, flavor_descr) |> 
  summarize(n_purchased = n(), .groups = 'drop') |> 
  group_by(region) |> 
  arrange(region, desc(n_purchased)) |>

  ungroup()

Results

Region appears to affect price as prices increases by 0.143 compared to the Central region. It appears most flavors price are lower than Cherry Garcia, the most popular flavor across all four regions. Indicating a higher price due to popular demand. It also appears using a coupon also increases the price by a small amount, indicating customers may likely be using coupons on higher priced ice-cream. However it is important to note that this model has a very high residual standard error of 0.659 and a low R2 of 0.029. This means only about 3% of the outcome is explained by the model.
